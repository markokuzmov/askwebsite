* OUR SPECIALTY

* STARTUPS

**Business 'migrants' (actual and potential E 2 visa holders)**  
  
If you are a foreign national treaty investor who plans to invest in a business in the United States or have already done so; by now you have probably realized how much “personalized”, or specific advice you will need for your particular situation.

Providing our services in English, Russian, Ukrainian and Polish.

If you have no need for an in-house bookkeeper, we will monitor your books to ensure you are meeting your profit objectives and complying with accepted accounting practices.

Making important business decisions on the type of entity you want to register and selecting a particular business model could be tricky. We can help you to make the correct decision.

We can also help you with securing a state or federal loan to help move your business ahead by aiding you in completing the necessary application paperwork. If needed we can help you to develop a business plan to support your request for additional funding.
