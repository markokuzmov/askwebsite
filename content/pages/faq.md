## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

### What do I need a bookkeeper for?

As small business owner you are probably much more enthusiastic and passionate about concentrating on what your business is about, rather than on keeping daily financial records. With even relatively small number of transactions, invoices, receipts and employees, things can easily slip through the cracks. Mistakes are almost unavoidable. All this keeps piling up.

Bookkeepers will take all this off your hands. They apply proper accounting methods in recording your finances and use professional software. They make sense of your numbers for you. Good bookkeeper is your partner in making sure your business is running smoothly.

### How does remote bookkeeping work?

You can work with us remotely choosing from a number of options. Pick one that works best for you.

* You can send us your statements and other paper work via e-mail and we'll reply likewise. Say, we'll work with your Quickbooks file on our system and send you the reports.
* You can upload your information to your password protected Personal Cabinet on this site and download information you need from us when it's ready.
* You can grant us access to your business bank accounts, Quickbooks file on your system or your Quickbooks on-line account.

  

If for some reason none of these optional are fully satisfactory, we'll be happy to find a way to accommodate you.
